          CONTROLS

          📱 Touch

          • Left thumb: hold anywhere on the left to drive and steer. Let go to roll to a stop, pull down to brake / reverse.
          • JUMP: tap to jump, tap again in the air to double jump.
          • Flip: swipe across JUMP in the direction you want to flip (up = forward).
          • BOOST: hold for nitro — it works in the air too.
          • DRIFT: powerslide; in the air it turns the stick into air roll.
          • Slide your thumb from BOOST onto JUMP to use both.

          ⌨️ Keyboard · 🎮 Controller

          • W/S: throttle / reverse, and nose down / up in the air
          • A/D: steer, and turn in the air
          • Hold E (air roll) in the air: A/D roll the car instead of turning
          • Space or right mouse: jump · Shift or left mouse: boost · Ctrl: powerslide
          • Jump twice with a direction held to flip · C: ball cam · R: look behind · Tab: scoreboard · 1-4: quick chat
          • Controller: RT/LT throttle · A jump · B boost · X powerslide + air roll · LB/RB roll · Y ball cam · right stick look · d-pad quick chat
          • Everything can be rebound in Settings → Bindings.

          🏆 Rules

          • Put the ball fully over the opponent's goal line.
          • Collect energy cells for boost (big = 100, small = 12).
          • At top speed (sparks trail) you wreck opponents you hit.
          • When time runs out the match ends as soon as the ball touches the ground. Tied? Overtime — next goal wins.
